About Us

We make game experiences with captivating stories and design.

Mendoan Games is a small indie game studio based in Indonesia, founded and led by Muhammad Al-Farabi Taufik and Nabil Marzuq Amirulzufar. As of now, we are still in the early stages of developing the studio, developing our skills and building our portfolio.

We are passionate about creating immersive and engaging game experiences with unique visuals and compelling ideas. All that came from our shared vision and never ending love for video games.
